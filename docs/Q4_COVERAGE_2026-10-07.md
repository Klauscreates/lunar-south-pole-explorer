# Q4 coverage and liquid-glass planner

## Scope

The existing Moon-first experience is retained. Planning panels have translucent glass surfaces, visual energy/visibility cards, a duration indicator, and October/November/December shortcuts. Custom windows may cross month boundaries.

Published coverage is 2026-10-01 00:00 UTC through 2027-01-01 00:00 UTC: 92 days, 13,249 ten-minute epochs per point, five points, 66,245 site epochs. The January boundary closes December 31; this is not general 2027 coverage. Unsupported dates remain unavailable.

## Regeneration and integrity

- NASA/JPL Horizons Sun/Earth observer tables were regenerated in bounded batches, preserving individual raw-response provenance and checking every timestamp for order and completeness.
- All five sites passed the local pinned NAIF SPICE comparison. Worst angular differences across the quarter: Sun 0.000164893 arcsec; Earth 0.001951894 arcsec. The two providers share JPL ephemeris lineage; agreement is not independent terrain or hardware validation.
- The original October 1–8 operational samples, window summaries, and CSV values retain their pre-SPICE regression hashes.
- Ten R1 attitude/environment scenarios per site were integrated continuously across the full interval. Selecting a later mission window does not reset its thermal state or impose a new landing-time initial condition.
- Native-terrain research reports and the geometric orbital scene were regenerated for the same epochs. Parent checksums and implementation hashes are checked before the build copies exports to the served directory.
- The near-field ray-input bound now permits two bodies for up to 20,000 epochs. Computation still works in bounded 128-ray batches; this change does not improve DEM resolution or fill missing coverage.

- Visual screenshots reviewed at phone size; date-panel header tightened to keep the primary action visible at 390 × 844.
- Touch regression passed for four-direction drags, orbital pan, pinch, exit, and controls. The local loading check observed controls after 1,000 ms, no speculative candidate downloads, and no repeated study downloads when reopening the planner.

## Unchanged scientific limits

Power remains modeled R1 generation with assumed thermal/environmental inputs. Battery icons indicate generating-time fraction, not stored charge. Communication cards report Earth-center geometric visibility, not RF link quality. Fine-terrain gaps remain Unknown; no measured boulder inventory or flight/landing certification was added. Orbital overview sizes/distances remain illustrative.

Browser-size/touch emulation is not physical iPhone Safari acceptance. The free hosting plan may still cold-start after inactivity.
